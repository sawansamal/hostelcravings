Policies & terms

The short, friendly version.

💵 Cash on delivery

  • All orders are Cash on Delivery (COD). UPI at the door is also accepted.
  • Please keep exact change ready where possible.
  • Repeated unpaid or refused orders may lead to your account being blocked.

🚪 Delivery

  • Door delivery: BS 12 residents.
  • Gate delivery: BS 11 & BS 13 residents.
  • Delivery usually takes 10–15 minutes. Absolutely no delivery charges.
  • If you're not at the door or gate, we'll try calling the phone number you entered.

❌ Cancellations

  • You can cancel any time before the order is marked 'Out for delivery' — just message us on WhatsApp.
  • We may cancel an order if an item goes out of stock; you'll be informed.

🔄 Returns & refunds

  • Damaged, expired or wrong items? Tell us at the time of delivery and we'll replace them or not charge you.
  • Opened food items can't be returned for hygiene reasons.

🏷️ Prices & stock

  • Prices shown are final and include everything — no delivery fee.
  • Stock is updated manually and may occasionally be off by a little.

📜 Terms of use

  • This is a small student-run shop for hostel residents.
  • By placing an order you agree to receive it and pay on delivery.
  • Your name, phone and room number are used only to deliver your order.
  • We may update these policies at any time.
